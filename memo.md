# Subject: Vireo Support Weekly Digest — what we found and what to act on

## 1. Business outcome

About **12–19 customers a week contact  again within 30 days about the same
issue** — roughly **₹40–68k of support cost per quarter** at today's volumes.
Cutting that by a quarter (fix it right the first time, show agents the
customer's device history) is worth **~₹10–17k per quarter**, and it directly
takes contacts out of the queue.

## 2. What customers are saying

Latest full week (22 Jun 2026, 199 tickets): delivery problems lead (45),
followed by charging/battery (29, up 14 on the week), returns/refunds (24),
billing (23), and connectivity (20). Charging/battery is the one moving —
worth a look at whether a recent batch or update is behind it.

## 3. What changed

Week-over-week: charging +14, returns +7, audio +7; billing −2, connectivity
−3. Email remains our worst first-response channel (company-wide about 1 in
11 tickets misses its response target; this week 15 of 199).

## 4. Operational finding

Yes — the "I already told your colleague" complaint is real in the data.
Same-model repeat contacts dominate (about 8 in 10 repeats concern the same
product), and several tickets literally re-open issues marked resolved days
earlier. The fix-first-time rate is the lever.

## 5. What i built

A command that produces this digest plus a fair agent leaderboard every week
from the ticket export — no platform, no per-ticket bills. The leaderboard
covers Tier-1 volume only — narrowed from the all-agents ask on purpose:
warranty cases are tracked separately because they take days by design and
can't be compared on weekly counts.

## 6. Confidence / limitations

Theme and repeat counts are estimates (about three-quarters of theme labels
and three-fifths of repeat flags check out on manual review), each backed by
ticket IDs you can click through. Counts were checked by a single reviewer
against the raw tickets, so treat them as estimates, not payroll truth.
Rupee figures use your policy's own contact
costs, and savings are stated as opportunity — realising them needs the
operational follow-through, which the weekly digest is built to track.
